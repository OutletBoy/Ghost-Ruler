Ghost Ruler

Lead a crew of friendly ghosts and spook a family out of their house before sunrise.

Version r2 · All versions ·

How to play

1Pick a ghost, then tap a room (or a room button) to send it there. The first trip is free, moves after that cost a little Belief.
2Use the ghost's quick scare or big scare. Fear fills each person's meter. At 70% they scream and spook the rooms next door. At 100% they run outside.
●Belief pays for scares, and scaring people earns it back. It also trickles in slowly.
?Everyone has a weak spot, Dark, Noise or Cold, that takes double fear. It shows after you scare them once.
★Some objects power up one scare type (they sparkle). The skeptic calms people and drains Belief while sharing a room with a ghost. The radio calms people. The last one left is extra jumpy.
⌨Drag to turn the house, pinch or scroll to zoom. Keys: 1 2 3 pick a ghost, Q quick scare, E big scare, Space pause.
