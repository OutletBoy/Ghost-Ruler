How to play

1Scout. The dotted trails show where each person goes tonight. Use a Peek to learn someone's weak spot.
2Plan. Pick 2 ghosts and tap a room to start each one there.
3Haunt. Tap a ghost, then tap a person for the scare wheel. Tap a room's floor to move there. Ghost cards at the bottom fire scares too.
●Glow pays for everything. You only earn it when someone crosses a fear line (25, 50, 75) or runs away. Spamming small scares goes broke.
Combos. Dark makes a room Gloomy (Noise hits harder). Cold makes it Frosty (Dark hits harder). Noise makes it Jumpy (people scream sooner). Screams close together make a chain that pays more.
!Uncle Morris doesn't believe in ghosts. Only two different scare types inside 4 seconds work on him, and he drains Glow while a ghost shares his room. Biscuit the dog barks at ghosts: Cold puts him to sleep, Noise scares him off.
!Ghost hunters. Big scares fill the Call meter. When it fills, Pip, Rosa and Teddy arrive. Meet one and you spin for it: a room effect, a ghost next door and Boost help you win. Lose to Teddy and you get jarred.
✋Drag to move the view, pinch to zoom, twist with two fingers (or the turn buttons) to go round. Double tap a room to zoom in. Hold on a person to see their card.
