Ghost Ruler

A new family moved into your house. Rude. Scare everyone out before dawn!

  • Pick a ghost, then tap a room to send it there.
  • Use its scares. Fear fills each person's meter. At full fear they run out the door.
  • Scares cost Belief, and scaring people earns it back.
  • Everyone has a weak spot (Dark, Noise or Cold) that takes double fear. Scare them once to find it.
  • Room objects boost one type of scare. Uncle Morris doesn't believe in you, and the radio calms people down. The last one left inside is extra jumpy.
